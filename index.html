Charlie

Charlie has read the whole book.

Charlie is the intelligence inside Wealthbook. Not a chatbot bolted to a dashboard — a reader who knows every page: what you own, what you spend, who your people are, and what your plan says. So when the spending and the plan disagree, Charlie notices. When a covenant on the Chapel matters to a decision about the Trust, Charlie has already turned to that page.

Maths, not vibes. Charlie doesn't guess from what the internet remembered last year. It works from your figures, current models, and legislation kept current to the second. Charlie will tell you what it knows, what it's estimating, and where it got it — every value confesses its source, and so does Charlie.

Not married to one model. Your data stays in your book, encrypted. Charlie takes the question, the context and the tools to whichever model is right for it today — and a better one tomorrow, without you noticing.

Yours to name, more or less. Charlie is whoever you want: she, he, or the machine in the corner. Ours is a she. Yours needn't be.

For advisers & firms

Keep the book. Invite the adviser.

The book belongs to the client and their family, for life. You are invited in: to the pages that concern you, for as long as you look after them. Advice is yours and regulated. The book is theirs.

Your firm's own record, what you did for them and when, sits behind your desk where the client never sees it, and hands over as evidence when someone asks. Nothing typed twice, nothing lost when they move on.
